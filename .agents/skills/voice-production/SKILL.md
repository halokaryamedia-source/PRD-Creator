## Voice Production judgment

### Naturalness
Preserve approved meaning, not accidental PRD syntax. Naturalness is register-specific.

### Actor / Character Continuity
For recurring Speakers, local emotion is a performance delta from a stable actor baseline. Do not use Audio Tags to force a voice outside its practical envelope or reinvent a character between Moments.

### Expression
Audio Tags are first-class Eleven v3 acting controls. Require Expression Coverage, not a fixed tag count.

### Language / Pronunciation
Critical names, terminology, acronyms, numbers/dates/symbols, and code-switched terms require an intentional spoken strategy when materially risky. Heard evidence is required for pronunciation approval.

### Generation continuity / variance
Connected narration may use adjacent context. A new request may reset acting state, so re-anchor material state when needed. One weak nondeterministic take does not prove a prompt defect; compare same-content candidates first.

## Voice Delivery judgment

Mechanical parity does not prove naturalness, actor continuity, expression, pronunciation, visual quality, or generated-audio quality.

Preparation may establish:

- Communication Conservation;
- Expression Conservation;
- Character Continuity Conservation when applicable;
- Pronunciation Conservation;
- Voice Script Readiness.

Actual audio quality and pronunciation approval still require heard evidence.
