# MIVUBI – Around The Block Voice Production
Version: 1.0.0
Mode: Preparation Draft
Language: Indonesian
Model: Eleven v3
Default Stability: Natural
Default Surface: Speech Synthesis
Enhance: OFF

> The narrative wording below is based on user-approved project dialogue. Commercial ElevenLabs voices have not been selected yet, so each speaker uses a Target Voice Profile. Formal Flow 5/6 handoff verification remains pending until the rendered PRD can be validated.

## Target Voice Profiles

- **Benteng Martello Guide** — Adult Indonesian voice; upbeat tourism-guide energy, friendly, clear, lightly playful, confident without sounding theatrical.
- **Plantation Guide** — Adult Indonesian voice; warm local host, proud and persuasive, conversational, with slightly stronger enthusiasm when talking about palm oil.
- **Old Man** — Older Indonesian male voice; warm, relaxed, slightly weathered, fatherly, humorous, natural everyday delivery.
- **Fisherman** — Adult Indonesian male voice; very relaxed, colloquial, dry humor, sounds focused on fishing rather than the chaos around him.
- **Zookeeper** — Young-to-middle adult Indonesian voice; friendly, casual, oddly calm, slightly sheepish, treats the emergency as routine.
- **Job Recruiter** — Adult Indonesian voice; cheerful, persuasive, optimistic, lightly sales-like, confident about the opportunity.

## 02. Perkebunan

### VO-FARM-01 — Farming Trend Invitation
Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
Speaker: Plantation Guide
Estimated Duration: 30–38 seconds

```performance
[proud, conversational]
Sekarang petani datang dari mana-mana. Ada juga wajah-wajah gagah berani yang entah bagaimana sekarang malah serius menekuni bidang ini.

[inviting]
Masa kamu enggak tertarik berkebun? Sekarang orang-orang sudah pada antre mau turun ke kebun, loh. Cobain deh, katanya seru.

Di sini ada kopi, kayu manis, sama sawit. Kopi bisa ditanam dan dipetik hasilnya. Kayu manis dirawat supaya nanti bisa dipanen lagi.

[slightly more excited]
Kalau sawit... nah, yang satu ini memang banyak yang suka. Katanya pohon bagus. Kalau masih ada tempat kosong, rasanya sayang kalau enggak ditanami.

[helpful]
Nanti hasil kebunnya bisa dijual. Uangnya bisa dipakai beli pupuk supaya tanaman lebih cepat tumbuh, atau disimpan kalau kamu punya keperluan lain.

[encouraging]
Sudah, coba saja dulu. Siapa tahu ternyata kamu juga punya bakat jadi petani.
```

## 03. Egrang

### VO-EGR-01 — Broken Road Solution
Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
Speaker: Old Man
Estimated Duration: 24–30 seconds

```performance
[casual]
Tadi waktu ke sini lewat jalan depan, kan? Rusak semua tuh.

Lubangnya banyak, becek juga. Kalau lewat situ mah siap-siap kena cipratan.

[helpful, amused]
Daripada repot-repot ngindarin jalan yang begitu, nih... coba pakai egrang.

Mainan zaman saya masih muda dulu. Enggak nyangka sekarang malah kepakai lagi.

[chuckles]
Yah, walaupun agak pegal... setidaknya enggak kena ciprat.

[playful]
Lumayan juga buat latihan keseimbangan. Daripada Yoga atau Pilates, sekalian aja pakai egrang.
```

## 05. Harimau Lepas

### VO-TIGER-01 — Zookeeper Request
Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
Speaker: Zookeeper
Estimated Duration: 31–39 seconds

```performance
[friendly]
Halo, Kak. Boleh bantu saya sebentar enggak?

Jadi ceritanya, saya habis nganter harimau dari kebun binatang.

[matter-of-fact]
Cuma ada sedikit masalah... harimaunya lepas di basement parkiran gedung sebelah.

[casual]
Sebenarnya saya mau urus sendiri, cuma saya lagi laper nih. Mau makan bakso dulu.

Sayang kalau ditinggal, nanti keburu dingin.

[asking politely]
Bisa tolong urusin sebentar, Kak? Jangan sampai dia keburu keluar ke jalan.

[reassuring, lightly amused]
Tenang aja, harimau sama kucing mah mirip-mirip tingkahnya.

Kalau sudah beres, balik lagi ke sini ya.

Nanti baksonya saya traktir.
```

### VO-TIGER-02 — After the Tiger
