# Around The Block Voice Requirements
Version: 1.0.0 Preview
Status: Approved Preview

Voice is used for story setup, character, humor/satire, and concise gameplay briefing. Exact wording/performance is owned by `work/voice-production.md`.

## VO-MAR-01 — Historical Experience Invitation
- Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
- Function: story_invitation
- Speaker: Benteng Martello Guide
- Trigger: First interaction before the match.
- Must communicate: invite the player to try the historical experience; keep the combat reveal limited.
- Must not add: full PvP rules before gameplay begins.

## VO-MAR-02 — Cannon Reveal
- Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
- Function: gameplay_reveal
- Speaker: Benteng Martello Guide
- Trigger: Match becomes active.
- Must communicate: take position, aim the cannon, become the last player standing.

## VO-FARM-01A — Farming Invitation
- Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
- Function: story_invitation
- Speaker: Plantation Guide
- Trigger: First interaction at the plantation.
- Must communicate: invite the player to farm and establish the farming-trend satire.
- Must not add: explicit real institution/person references.

## VO-FARM-01B — Crops & Farming
- Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
- Function: gameplay_brief
- Speaker: Plantation Guide
- Trigger: After the player accepts the invitation.
- Must communicate: Coffee, Cinnamon, Palm Oil, harvest selling, Fertilizer, and saving currency.

## VO-EGR-01 — Broken Road Solution
- Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
- Function: story_and_gameplay_setup
- Speaker: Old Man
- Trigger: Interaction near the damaged road.
- Must communicate: damaged/muddy road, egrang as the solution, nostalgic joke, Yoga/Pilates punchline.
- Must not add: balance meter or special falling mechanic.

## VO-FISH-01A — Fishing Invitation
- Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
- Function: story_invitation
- Speaker: Fisherman
- Trigger: First interaction at the pond.
- Must communicate: invite the player to relax and fish; establish the fisherman's detached attitude toward the surrounding chaos.
- Must not add: mockery aimed at displaced people.

## VO-FISH-01B — Fisherman Banter
- Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
- Function: character_banter
- Speaker: Fisherman
- Trigger: After the player starts fishing.
- Must communicate: conversational fishing banter and the absurd Hiu catch joke.

## VO-TIGER-01 — A Small Job
- Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
- Function: job_offer
- Speaker: Businessman
- Trigger: Businessman calls the player before the basement encounter.
- Must communicate: paid job, escaped pet described only as a big “cat”, indifference to the method, Black Briefcase reward cue.
- Must not add: the word tiger/macan before the basement reveal; Zookeeper/Bakso story.

## VO-TIGER-02 — Payment
- Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
- Function: payment
- Speaker: Businessman
- Trigger: Player returns after resolving the tiger encounter.
- Must communicate: promised payment and the arrogant transactional closing tone.

## VO-MINE-01 — Job Offer
- Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
- Function: job_offer
- Speaker: Job Recruiter
- Trigger: Player meets the recruiter before travelling to the mining area.
- Must communicate: outside-island job, easy-work/high-pay pitch, optimistic success promise.
- Must not add: paid progression details too early.

## VO-MINE-02 — Career Progression
- Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
- Function: progression_explanation
- Speaker: Job Recruiter
- Trigger: Player arrives at the mining area.
- Must communicate: three-tier career progression, paid access, work/save/pay loop, “mau sukses ya harus modal dulu” landing.
- Must not add: exact prices, mineral names, extra tiers.

## VO-DEALER-01 — Installment by Parts
- Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
- Function: purchase_explanation
- Speaker: Dealer NPC
- Trigger: First interaction with the Flying Motorcycle Dealer.
- Must communicate: Flying Motorcycle is purchased/assembled per part; preserve the “dicicil... per bagian / iya, bagiannya” satire.
- Must not add: claims that a real regulation requires this system.

## Production Rules
- Model: Eleven v3.
- Default Stability: Natural.
- Exact performance tags remain in English inside the prompt.
- Dialogue remains Indonesian.
- Long dialogue may be split into multiple clips when needed for natural delivery.
