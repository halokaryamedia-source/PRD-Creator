# Voice Requirements
Project: MIVUBI – Around The Block
Source PRD revision: 1.0.0
Voice system: Direct NPC narration in Indonesian. Each gameplay uses concise contextual narration; no dialogue tree is required.

> Status note: The narrative meaning below has been explicitly approved in the project discussion. Formal rendered PRD handoff verification is still pending because the current execution channel cannot run the repository renderer.

## Benteng Martello

### VO-MAR-01 — Historical Experience Invitation
Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
Function: briefing
Speaker: Benteng Martello Guide
Channel: Direct NPC narration before the player joins the activity.
Trigger: Player interacts with the Benteng Martello guide before the match.
Purpose: Make the activity sound like an inviting historical experience without revealing the full absurdity too early.
Must communicate:
- Ask whether the player has imagined living in the colonial era.
- Encourage the player to try the experience.
- Encourage bringing at least one other player.
- Reassure the player that the experience is prepared.
Must not add/repeat:
- Do not explain Naval Combat, DeathTime, shore cannons, sharks, or Last Player Standing here.
- Do not turn the line into a tutorial.

### VO-MAR-02 — Cannon Reveal
Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
Function: reveal
Speaker: Benteng Martello Guide
Channel: Activity narration when gameplay begins.
Trigger: Benteng Martello match becomes active and the player is positioned for combat.
Purpose: Reveal that the “historical experience” is actually a combat simulation and give one immediate action.
Must communicate:
- Take position.
- Aim the cannon at the opponent.
- Become the last player standing.
Must not add/repeat:
- Do not explain all match rules.
- Do not add historical exposition.

## Perkebunan

### VO-FARM-01 — Farming Trend Invitation
Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
Function: briefing
Speaker: Plantation Guide
Channel: Direct NPC narration at the plantation.
Trigger: Player interacts with the plantation narrator.
Purpose: Establish the farming satire, introduce the three commodities, and explain the simple harvest/economy loop.
Must communicate:
- Farming is becoming popular among people from unexpected backgrounds.
- Invite the player to try farming.
- Introduce coffee, cinnamon, and palm oil.
- Palm oil receives noticeably stronger enthusiasm.
- Harvest can be sold.
- Currency can buy fertilizer or be saved.
Must not add/repeat:
- Do not identify a real institution or person.
- Do not make the satire explicit.
- Do not add another crop or economy system.

## Egrang

### VO-EGR-01 — Broken Road Solution
Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
Function: briefing
Speaker: Old Man
Channel: Direct NPC narration near the egrang activity.
Trigger: Player interacts with the old man near the damaged road.
Purpose: Explain the broken-road joke and invite the player to use egrang.
Must communicate:
- The road is damaged, muddy, and likely to splash the player.
- Egrang is an old toy from the speaker's youth.
- It unexpectedly becomes useful now.
- It may be tiring but avoids splashes.
- It is also jokingly presented as balance exercise instead of Yoga/Pilates.
Must not add/repeat:
- Do not claim there is a balance meter or falling mechanic.
- Do not turn the line into movement instructions.

## Mancing di Empang

### VO-FISH-01 — Fishing During the Chaos
Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
Function: briefing
Speaker: Fisherman
Channel: Direct NPC narration at the pond.
Trigger: Player interacts with the fisherman.
Purpose: Establish the satire of ignoring the nearby eviction and invite the player to relax by fishing.
Must communicate:
- Invite a tired/stressed player to sit and fish.
- Mention the eviction/noise nearby.
- Dismiss it as someone else's business.
- Focus on the fishing.
- Mention that the pond may produce a shark.
- Admit the speaker does not know why.
Must not add/repeat:
- Do not explain custom fishing systems because gameplay uses vanilla fishing.
- Do not make the eviction itself the joke.

## Harimau Lepas

### VO-TIGER-01 — Zookeeper Request
Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
Function: briefing
Speaker: Zookeeper
Channel: Direct NPC narration on the main road.
Trigger: Player interacts with the Zookeeper before the tiger encounter is completed.
Purpose: Explain the escaped tiger emergency while keeping the Zookeeper absurdly calm and focused on eating bakso.
Must communicate:
- The Zookeeper recently transported a tiger from the zoo.
- The tiger escaped into the nearby building basement.
- The Zookeeper wants to eat bakso first because it will get cold.
- Ask the player to handle the tiger before it gets outside.
- Reassure the player by comparing tiger behavior to a cat.
- Promise to treat the player to bakso afterward.
Must not add/repeat:
- Do not introduce a complex escape timer or path system.
- Do not make the Zookeeper panic.

### VO-TIGER-02 — After the Tiger
Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
Function: completion
Speaker: Zookeeper
Channel: Direct NPC narration after the encounter.
Trigger: Player returns/interacts after the tiger has been validly defeated.
Purpose: Acknowledge the player's help and land the recurring-tiger joke.
Must communicate:
- Thank the player.
- The Zookeeper is tired of the tiger escaping repeatedly.
Must not add/repeat:
- Do not replay the original briefing.
- Do not invent a new reward line.

## CyberWorld Mining

### VO-MINE-01 — Job Offer
Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
Function: briefing
Speaker: Job Recruiter
Channel: Direct NPC narration at the city job-offer point.
Trigger: Player interacts with the recruiter before taking the mining job.
Purpose: Sell the outside-island mining job as an easy, high-paying opportunity during a difficult job market.
Must communicate:
- Ask whether the player is looking for work.
- Offer a job outside the island.
- Describe it as easy and well paid.
- Contrast it with the difficulty of finding work locally.
- Invite the player to follow.
- Suggest the player might return successful.
Must not add/repeat:
- Do not explain the paid career-access system yet.
- Do not list all mining mechanics.

### VO-MINE-02 — Career Progression
Type: Direct NPC Dialogue
Function: reveal
Speaker: Job Recruiter
Channel: Direct NPC narration after arrival in the mining area.
Trigger: Player arrives at the mining job and receives the progression explanation.
Purpose: Explain the first mining tier and reveal the absurd paid “career progression” system.
Must communicate:
- Welcome the player to the new job.
- Start from the first island and mine resources.
- Better earnings allow access to the next island.
- Career progression is described as clear.
- Moving up requires an administration/access fee.
- The player must work, collect money, and pay to move up.
- Land on the joke that success requires capital.
Must not add/repeat:
- Do not invent exact access prices.
- Do not invent resource names.
- Do not add extra mining tiers.
